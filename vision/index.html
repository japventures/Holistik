01

One way to make food better

BetterBasics

Improve what doesn’t need reinventing.

Some foods don’t need to be reinvented. They just need to be better.

Quality. Sourcing. Ingredients. Formulation. Transparency. Convenience. Experience. The opportunity can be found anywhere people deserve a simpler, better choice.

Simple doesn’t mean basic.

Sometimes innovation means doing less—and doing it exceptionally well.
02

One way to make food better

BetterAlternatives

Take something people already know, love or understand—and rethink the experience.

Familiar occasion.
Different approach.

Chia
Overnights

A familiar ritual, reimagined around a different ingredient.

Simple.Convenient.Naturally nutritious.Made for real life.

People already understand overnight oats. What if we rethink the experience around chia?

A refrigerated, ready-to-eat breakfast or snack built around chia’s natural qualities, great ingredients, real flavors and convenience.

Why it belongs here

Chia can be naturally nutritious without turning this into a “functional food.” This is Better Alternatives because HOLISTIK transforms an ingredient into a ready-to-eat solution for a familiar occasion.

Can we make it better without taking away what people love?

Not a diet version. Not a substitute. Not an artificial “healthy” replacement. Keep what makes the experience great. Rethink what could make it genuinely better.

Keep what people love.

Rethink what can be better.

One way to make food better

BetterFunctional

Use nutrition, science and formulation when they can solve a real need.

When food can do more, function should have a purpose.

Not functionality for the label. Not ingredients for the claim. Real food, useful science and thoughtful processes—applied only when they make everyday life meaningfully better.

Function with purpose.

Nutrition designed around a real everyday need.
